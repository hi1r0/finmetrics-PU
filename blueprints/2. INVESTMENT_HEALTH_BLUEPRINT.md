## 2. Sector Operating Archetypes (`modules/sector_profiles.py`)

A single fundamental scoring model produces false positives when comparing capital-intensive steel producers with asset-light software exporters. The suite codifies 5 specialized sector archetypes:

| Sector | Operating Archetype | Core Fundamental DNA | Key Diagnostic Ratios | Primary Valuation Anchor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Information Technology** | Asset-Light Human Capital | High cash conversion, negative net debt, premium ROCE, zero working capital drag | `operating_margin`, `fcf_margin`, `roce`, `receivables_turnover` | P/E Ratio |
| **FMCG** | Consumer Franchise & Brand Moat | High gross margins, rapid inventory velocity, negative cash cycles, resilient ROE | `gross_margin`, `inventory_turnover`, `fcf_margin`, `roe` | P/E Ratio |
| **Pharmaceuticals** | R&D & Regulated Manufacturing | Elevated gross margins, patent protections, moderate capital intensity, export quality | `gross_margin`, `roce`, `operating_margin`, `asset_turnover` | EV / EBITDA |
| **Automobile** | Operating Leverage & Supply Chain | Moderate gross margins, cyclical capex, sensitive to supplier working capital | `operating_margin`, `inventory_turnover`, `asset_turnover`, `interest_coverage` | EV / EBITDA |
| **Metals & Mining** | Capital-Intensive Commodity Cycle | Low asset turnover, heavy fixed capital, high debt sensitivity, volatile margins | `operating_margin`, `asset_turnover`, `debt_to_equity`, `interest_coverage` | EV / EBITDA |

## 3. Quantitative Financial Health Models (`modules/health_models.py`)

### 3.1. Piotroski F-Score (9-Point Fundamental Accounting Quality)
Evaluates fundamental accounting signals across 3 categories (0 to 9 scale):
- **Profitability (4 Points):**
  1. $P_1$: Net Income $> 0$
  2. $P_2$: Operating Cash Flow $> 0$
  3. $P_3$: Return on Assets (ROA) expansion YoY
  4. $P_4$: Quality of Earnings ($CFO > PAT$, accrual check)
- **Leverage, Liquidity & Sources of Funds (3 Points):**
  5. $L_1$: Long-Term Debt / Total Assets decreased YoY
  6. $L_2$: Current Ratio improved YoY
  7. $L_3$: No substantial share dilution (Shares Outstanding held firm)
- **Operating Efficiency (2 Points):**
  8. $E_1$: Gross Margin expansion YoY
  9. $E_2$: Asset Turnover improvement YoY

**Classification:**
- **8–9 Points:** *High Quality (Tier 1)* — robust execution and earnings quality.
- **5–7 Points:** *Moderate Quality* — stable operations, selective monitoring.
- **0–4 Points:** *Weak / Low Quality* — deteriorating balance sheet and earnings quality.

---

### 3.2. Edward Altman Z''-Score (Emerging Markets & Non-Financials)
Designed specifically for emerging markets and non-financial corporates to eliminate reliance on volatile market capitalization multiples:
$$Z'' = 6.56 X_1 + 3.26 X_2 + 6.72 X_3 + 1.05 X_4$$

Where:
- $X_1 = \frac{\text{Working Capital}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (short-term liquidity cushion)
- $X_2 = \frac{\text{Retained Earnings}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (cumulative profitability)
- $X_3 = \frac{\text{Operating EBIT}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (asset earning power)
- $X_4 = \frac{\text{Book Value of Equity}}{\text{Total Liabilities}}$ (solvency gearing)

**Distress Zones:**
- **$Z'' > 2.60$:** **Safe Zone** (Robust solvency cushion; low statistical distress risk).
- **$1.10 \le Z'' \le 2.60$:** **Grey Zone** (Moderate posture; monitor working capital & debt).
- **$Z'' < 1.10$:** **Distress Zone** (Elevated solvency distress; thin liquidity buffer).

---

### 3.3. DuPont 3-Stage ROE Decomposition
Deconstructs Return on Equity (ROE) into operational efficiency, capital velocity, and financial leverage:
$$\text{ROE} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Income}}{\text{Total Revenue}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Total Revenue}}{\text{Total Assets}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Total Equity}}\right)$$
$$\text{ROE} = \text{Net Profit Margin} \times \text{Asset Turnover} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}$$

**Primary Driver Diagnosis:**
- **Margin Power (Pricing Moat):** Net Margin $\ge 18\%$, indicating customer pricing power.
- **Asset Velocity (Capital Efficiency):** Asset Turnover $\ge 1.2\times$, driving returns via volume.
- **Financial Leverage (Gearing Dependent):** Equity Multiplier $\ge 2.5\times$, signaling vulnerability to interest rate cycles.

---

### 3.4. Valuation Overlay & Posture
Compares company multiples (P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, FCF Yield) against the sector peer medians:
- **Deep Value (Quality at a Discount):** Trades at $>25\%$ discount to sector median while maintaining high quality (Piotroski $\ge 7$, Altman Safe Zone).
- **Valuation Trap (Cheap but Distressed):** Trades at low multiples but exhibits severe fundamental deterioration (Piotroski $\le 3$ or Altman Distress Zone).
- **Frothy Valuation:** Trades at $>35\%$ premium to sector peers.
- **Fair Value:** Trades within $\pm 15\%$ of sector medians.

---

### 3.5. Consolidated Company Health Verdict
Synthesizes all quantitative signals into an actionable institutional badge:
- **`PASS` (Green):** Strong fundamental health across solvency, earnings quality, and cash generation.
- **`WATCH` (Orange):** Mixed profile; monitor leverage, valuation premium, or working capital.
- **`RED_FLAG` (Red):** Elevated distress risk, low accounting quality, or valuation trap.

## 4. Macro Sector Compass (`modules/sector_compass.py`)

Aggregates individual company health checks into a macro sector ranking:
- **Attractiveness Score (0 to 100):**
  $$\text{Score} = 0.40 \cdot \text{Median Health} + 0.30 \cdot \text{Quality Breadth \%} + 0.20 \cdot \text{Distress Buffer} + 0.10 \cdot \text{Cash Flow Quality \%}$$
- **Postures:**
  - *Prime Quality / Overweight* (Score $\ge 75$, Distress $\le 10\%$)
  - *Neutral / Selective Quality* (Score $\ge 60$)
  - *Defensive / High Dispersion* (Score $< 60$)

## 5. User Interface Architecture (`app.py`)

The Streamlit dashboard is organized into 5 institutional workflows:
1. **🧭 Sector Compass:** Cross-sector macro attractiveness leaderboard, quality breadth vs. distress quadrant, and sector operating archetype cards.
2. **🩺 Company Health Diagnostics:** Company selector, verdict badge (`PASS` / `WATCH` / `RED_FLAG`), Piotroski 9-signal checklist, Altman Z'' meter, DuPont 3-stage breakdown, valuation gap table, and SWOT matrix.
3. **🏆 Sector Leaderboard & Screener:** Interactive multi-criteria screener (verdicts, F-score, distress zones, multiples) paired with an on-demand Gemini AI executive briefing using key rotation.
4. **📊 Peer Benchmarking & Outliers:** Ratio distribution boxplots, peer statistics heatmaps, radar charts, and IQR/Isolation Forest anomaly detection.
5. **📥 Audit & Export:** One-click generation of comprehensive Excel workbooks and CSV summary datasets.
