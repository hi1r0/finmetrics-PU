\chapter{Conclusion and Future Enhancements}
\label{ch:conclusion}

\section{Final Outcomes}
The \textit{FinMetrics Suite} successfully addresses key challenges in quantitative financial analysis, outlier detection, and public equity peer benchmarking:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Its standardizer successfully resolves statement naming variations across companies, building consistent peer datasets automatically.
    \item By screening and masking statistical outliers, the system protects peer group medians and standard deviations, establishing objective comparative baselines.
    \item Integrating bounded 5-pillar operational scoring, rule-based SWOT matrices, and Gemini-driven interactive diagnostics allows equity analysts to easily translate raw financial metrics into clear, actionable corporate performance diagnostics.
\end{itemize}

\section{Contributions}
The primary contributions of this project include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Standardized Financial Parsing Engine}: Implemented a 60-field synonym resolution dictionary resolving reporting nomenclature disparities across listed public corporate filings.
    \item \textbf{Outlier-Resistant Benchmarks}: Designed a baseline mapping process that masks outlier cells before calculating descriptive peer statistics, protecting peer medians and quartiles from extreme data points.
    \item \textbf{Bounded Multi-Pillar Scoring Architecture}: Designed a transparent 0-100 Sigmoid scoring model and contribution matrix aggregating 5 operational pillars.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Future Scope}
While the platform provides robust analytical performance, several enhancements are planned for future versions:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Automated Regulatory Filing Scraping}: Build automated ingestion pipelines and web parsers for regulatory filing repositories (e.g., SEC EDGAR and MCA corporate disclosure portals), streamlining direct extraction from annual reports.
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Sector Alerting}: Implement automated alert systems that monitor selected sectors and notify analysts when a company's ratios trigger extreme outlier or solvency warnings.
    \item \textbf{Predictive Analytics}: Integrate predictive time-series models (such as ARIMA or Prophet) to forecast ratio trends, providing forward-looking strategic insights.
    \item \textbf{Cross-Market Coverage}: Expand peer benchmarking beyond domestic equity markets to international exchanges, enabling cross-market comparative analytics and global sector baselines.
\end{itemize}
